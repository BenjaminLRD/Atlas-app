## Word-for-Word Walkthrough Script

### 1. Introduction (0:00 – 0:15)
> *"Most fitness apps are dumb digital logbooks: they make you type numbers into spreadsheets and hand out generic PDF routines. They completely fail to answer the most important question: 'How should I train and eat today based on my recovery, sleep, and historical fatigue?'*  
> *Meet **Atlas** — the autonomous AI personal trainer and digital athlete platform."*

### 2. Conversational AI Coach & Long-Term Memory (0:15 – 0:35)
> *(Navigate to **AI Coach Screen**)*  
> *"Atlas is powered by a hybrid architecture combining Google Gemini 2.0 with on-device machine learning. Notice how the Coach already knows my athlete profile: I'm Alex, 26, focused on Hypertrophy. My recovery score is 88 today, and I hit 185g of protein yesterday.*  
> *Atlas remembers my past shoulder pain, automatically replacing dangerous movements without me having to re-explain my history."*

### 3. Adaptive Workout Autoregulation (0:35 – 0:55)
> *(Navigate to **Workout Session HUD**)*  
> *"When I launch my Upper Push session, Atlas isn't following a static plan. It continuously autoregulates my working sets based on live RPE and historical fatigue.  
> If my recovery is high, it prescribes a progressive overload milestone. If my bar speed drops, it dynamically trims volume to prevent central nervous system burnout."*

### 4. Multimodal Food Vision & Barcode Scanning (0:55 – 1:15)
> *(Navigate to **Food Scanner Screen**)*  
> *"Logging food shouldn't take five minutes. With our multimodal vision scanner, athletes simply snap a photo of their plate or scan a barcode.  
> Our computer vision pipeline instantly estimates portions, calories, and macronutrients, immediately syncing to their daily protein target."*

### 5. Real-Time Form Biomechanics (1:15 – 1:35)
> *(Navigate to **Exercise Form Coach Screen**)*  
> *"Atlas turns any smartphone into a biomechanical lab. Using on-device MediaPipe 33-point pose landmark estimation, the camera tracks joint angles at 30 frames per second.  
> On squats, it verifies depth parallel, flags knee valgus collapse, and gives sub-second auditory feedback to ensure injury-free lifting."*

### 6. Predictive Analytics & Digital Athlete Twin (1:35 – 1:50)
> *(Navigate to **Product Showcase / Showcase Dashboard**)*  
> *"All of this data feeds the **Digital Athlete Twin** — a mathematical model projecting 4, 8, and 12-week 1RM compound strength curves.  
> It accurately forecasts that my Bench Press will reach 118kg in 8 weeks with a 94% probability, giving athletes crystal-clear visibility into their trajectory."*

### 7. Closing & Community (1:50 – 2:00)
> *(Navigate to **Ranked Leagues Screen**)*  
> *"Layered with competitive ranked leagues, gamified XP, and an offline-first Supabase PostgreSQL backend, Atlas represents the future of personal fitness intelligence. Thank you!"*
