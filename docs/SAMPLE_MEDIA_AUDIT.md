## Format and size

- Videos are H.264 (main profile) MP4, 4 to 15 seconds, silent, 368 to 854 pixels wide, with `+faststart`. MP4/H.264 plays on Android (ExoPlayer) and iOS (AVPlayer). The earlier WebM files did not play on iOS.
- Photos are progressive JPEG, at most 1100 pixels on the long side and under 240 KB each.
- Clips were trimmed and re-encoded for size; the content is unchanged otherwise.
