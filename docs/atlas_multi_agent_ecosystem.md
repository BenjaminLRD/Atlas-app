# Atlas Multi-Agent AI Coaching Ecosystem

## 1. Multi-Agent Collaborative Architecture

Rather than relying on a single monolithic prompt, Atlas deploys a committee of specialized autonomous agents that deliberate, cross-examine proposals, and synthesize balanced coaching strategies.

```
                           [ Athlete Telemetry & Context ]
                                          │
```

## 2. Agent Interaction & Deliberation Protocols

### 2.1 Domain Responsibilities
- **Training Agent**: Optimizes muscle hypertrophy and 1RM strength progression.
- **Nutrition Agent**: Regulates energy balance and ensures protein synthesis thresholds ($\ge 1.6\text{g/kg}$).
- **Recovery Agent**: Monitors parasympathetic status; maintains veto power on overtraining.
- **Behavior Agent**: Preserves consistency and habit streaks.
- **Research Agent**: Grounds recommendations in current peer-reviewed literature.

### 2.2 Consensus & Dispute Resolution
- **Rule 1 (Recovery Dominance)**: If the Recovery Agent rates systemic fatigue as Critical ($>8/10$ urgency), its deload veto overrides standard Training Agent volume progression.
- **Rule 2 (Behavior Compromise)**: When a training session is vetoed for recovery, the Behavior Agent substitutes a low-strain mobility session to maintain habit momentum.
